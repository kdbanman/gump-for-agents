A living face is a little emptier for every way round it, by an amount that falls smoothly as the way round lengthens and does not end. A tube’s discount in price per cell was times that emptiness on tubes up to 84 cells; the long tubes your card has measured would need .

A wrapped face is a face of the box closed on itself like a torus: walk far enough across it and you are back where you started. Each way of getting back is a loop, a vector of the torus’s period lattice; its length is written as √loop², the number the ledger uses being the loop’s squared length in lattice steps. A face with exactly one loop much shorter than the others is a tube, and the length of that loop is its girth. The price per cell is how much the logarithm of a face’s mean lifetime grows for each cell added, which for a face with no short loop is about one fifth.
