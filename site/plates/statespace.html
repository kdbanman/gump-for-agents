A seed sees as many states as it lives generations: about 20.30·A for a face of A cells, against one bit per cell of the room. The count grows exponentially, and the share it sees shrinks exponentially.

“…how much of the possible state space a Gump seed explores. … For the longest lived family of seed states (which I think are the random seeds), how many of the possible states are hit? And is there any consistency or pattern that extrapolates as size goes up?”
