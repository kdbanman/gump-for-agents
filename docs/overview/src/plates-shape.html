At equal area, a periodic face lives longer the longer its shortest way round.

So every price per cell measured on a periodic slab, including the 0.21 on the area-law plate, carries a wrap-around term, and the shortest loop is not the whole of it: at 60 cells two tori with the same shortest loop still differ. Not known: how long a wide face with no wrap-around lives. Hull faces have none, but they have edges, and nothing yet connects the two numbers.
