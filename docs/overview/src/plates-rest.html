      What is not settled, and what is running.

      1. The constant. One price per cell for every wide face is allowed anywhere from 0.207 to 0.219. The GTX 1080 in your house now runs the GPU work: equilateral tori at 93 cells, the decisive one, are running on it; hull faces of 108 cells with 10⁴ deaths come next. Its first calibration found that the 4 × 4 torus value on file was a high draw (9.9324; now 9.9249 from three codes). running
      2. The 5 × 5 anchor and the 5 × 5 patch. The 5 × 5 torus value that anchors the constant’s forecasts has one measurement; it is to be re-measured on the GTX 1080. The 4 × 5 empty patch is settled (CPU and GPU 1.4σ apart); from 5 × 5 on the patches are GPU only. queued
      3. Symmetric collapse, what is left. The edge ladder is measured; three things are not: how much of the √A term is a mirror’s (up to about a third is not excluded), whether the rim term bends (faces with diagonal mirrors at 759 sites would tell, on the GPU), and whether the factor is exactly one sixth. Dying tied faces have now been watched (“A face dying, watched”). open
      4. Dying faces at other sizes. A third round of the dying-face study (the same dips at other sizes) has reported and is under review; nothing from it is shown here yet. under review
      5. Why 0.21, and why 0.099. Both prices live in five layers of the rule over coin flips; neither is derived, nor their ratio of one half. open
      6. A guard for the GPU. Written, and checked on the CPU against about 7,600 planted lives with cycles of period 1 to 12,345: it recognised no chaotic life and missed no cycle. One claim did not hold (for long odd periods it can be slow to notice), so guarded runs must report the time it spends noticing. Five corrections are asked of the GTX 1080 session before its first results are merged. built, checked on the CPU
      7. The rows nobody had re-run. An auditor re-ran 28 ledger rows that only their producer had ever run: 26 matched, and twenty moved up a grade; ten remain. One number changed: the correlation length of the chaos is 0.65 ± 0.03 of a cube, not 0.68 ± 0.01 (the fit reached too far and its error bars were too small). done 15 November
