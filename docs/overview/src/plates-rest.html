      What is not settled, and what is running.

      1. The constant. One price per cell for every wide face is allowed anywhere from 0.207 to 0.219. The runs that could narrow it (hull faces of 108 cells with 10⁴ deaths; equilateral tori, 93 cells the decisive one) need the GPU, whose budget this month is nearly spent: they wait on your word. waiting
      2. Symmetric collapse. A theory line is trying to derive a symmetric face’s one sixth, its √A term and the factor 1.5 between centres from the asymmetric law. Forecasts first, CPU only. running
      3. The 4 × 5 empty patch. The CPU and GPU disagree by about 2σ; the CPU is rerunning it ten times longer. The last price per cell of the patch plate rests on it. running
      4. Why 0.21, and why 0.099. Both prices live in five layers of the rule over coin flips; neither is derived, nor their ratio of one half. open
      5. A guard for the GPU. Before any further GPU lifetime run, its drivers must catch settled cycles of any period. Written down, not built: it cannot be tested without the GPU. owed
