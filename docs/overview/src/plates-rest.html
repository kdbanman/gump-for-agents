      Open now

      What is not settled, and what is running.

      1. Can gump compute? It has wires, pulses, a gate where one pulse blocks another, a counter and a clock of any length. It has no corner, no crossing, no gate with an output line of its own, and no mirror that sends pulse after pulse back. A third push is building a machine from the parts: an emitter that repeats, a clock driving a counter, a corner. running
      2. The signals, reviewed. The review of the first push has reported and is being recorded. The review of the second is running, the two proofs first: the clock at every length, the tape for every word. Until then those two claims rest on their author. under review
      3. One price per cell, or two. Your GTX 1080 is running wrapped faces of 81, 84, 108 and 111 cells, then a 108-cell face with its rim. The forecast for the four is the straight line; the 81-cell face alone tells the line from every smooth curve. If the rimmed face keeps climbing, a face with a living rim and a wrapped one have different prices. running
      4. The state space, what is left. The counts are reviewed; where the count turns from a handful to exponential (faces of 30 to 40 cells) is not seen, nor are hulls wider than 6 layers. Your side-16 cube is counted from its one run. open
      5. A dying face’s last cells. Whether a nearly empty face keeps a core of fixed density cannot be tested on faces small enough to be caught that low. open
      6. A clock whose period divides by four. Every built clock has period 4N + 2; none of 4N was found, and no reason is known. open
      7. Why 0.21, and why 0.099. Both prices live in five layers of the rule over coin flips; neither is derived, nor their ratio of one half. open
