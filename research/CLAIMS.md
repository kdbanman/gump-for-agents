# Claims ledger

The headline claims that `README.md`, `docs/WHAT-I-SEE.md`, `research/STATUS.md` and the web tour (`web/src/tour.js`)
rest on, each with where it is recorded, how to reproduce it, and how well it has been checked. Started by the
auditor on 2026-11-13 (`research/AUDIT-2026-11-13.md` has the findings); **keep it current**: when a claim is added
to the README/essay/status/tour, add a row; when a check is re-run, update its level and date.

**Tag**: as recorded — proved / measured / conjecture (forecasts are conjectures).
**Level**: **A** = independently re-derived by different code or a different agent (named); **B** = re-run or
recomputed by someone other than the producer and matched (statistical: within the stated error): the auditor on
2026-11-13 for the rows of that date, the named reviewer or the lead for later rows (the date is in the last
column); **B−** = as B, but what was recomputed is the committed output of runs on one GPU implementation that no
second implementation has repeated at the claimed sizes, and whose execution on the GPU could not be checked (used
by the reviews of GPU pull requests 8 and 12; counted as B in the tally); **C** = has a command, not re-run;
**D** = no way to reproduce given. "B (table)" = re-derived from the committed curated table, not from raw runs.
**Theorem ids** are the ledger's own and agree with `docs/AUTOMATON.md` only for T1–T4: AUTOMATON's T5 (threshold
lemma) is ledger **T8**, its T6 (sealed sets) is ledger **T9**; ledger T5 and T6 have no AUTOMATON number. In the
notebook, `research/theory/` and `research/toy2d/`, "T5" and "T6" mean AUTOMATON's. **K9–K11** were numbered K1–K3
until 2026-11-14, a second use of those ids; K1–K3 are the thickness rows.
Commands run from the repo root; `G=engine/target/release/gump`. `check.sh` = covered by `research/check.sh`.
